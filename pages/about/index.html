Our Institution

A legacy built on education and values.

R.K. Arya College is a student-focused institution committed to academic excellence, character development and meaningful contribution to society.

Maharishi Dayanand Saraswati, in pursuance of Vedic values of tolerance, compassion, sharing and respect, founded Arya Samaj on April 10, 1875, at Mumbai and subsequently established its work in Lahore in 1877.

His educational philosophy placed strong emphasis on knowledge, character development and the physical, moral and social progress of humanity. The movement promoted educational institutions such as Vedic schools and Gurukuls where education was closely connected with values, culture and responsible citizenship.

Inspired by these principles and recognizing the need for an institution of higher learning in Nawanshahr Doaba, dedicated Arya Samajists came together to advance the cause of education in the region.

Our History

From humble beginnings to an institution of excellence.

More than seven decades of educational service, growth and community impact.

1952 Established

The foundation of R.K. Arya College

As a result of their dedication to the cause of education, Radha Krishan Arya College was founded in the year 1952.

A great philanthropist, Dr. Radha Krishan, donated a major share of his property to support the institution, and the college was named in his honour.

Since its foundation, the college has continued to expand its educational offerings while remaining committed to providing accessible and affordable higher education to students from Nawanshahr and surrounding rural communities.

Educational Philosophy

Education is more than knowledge.

We believe education should prepare students not only for professional success, but also for responsible participation in society.

The philosophy of Maharishi Dayanand Saraswati placed education at the centre of character development. Knowledge was viewed as a means through which an individual could develop the ability to distinguish truth, act responsibly and contribute positively to society.

R.K. Arya College continues this tradition by bringing together academic learning, values, discipline, cultural awareness and opportunities for personal development.

Academic Affiliation

Connected to a wider academic ecosystem.

R.K. Arya College is affiliated with Guru Nanak Dev University, Amritsar, for its undergraduate and postgraduate programmes.

Through this academic association, the institution continues to provide structured higher education across multiple disciplines while supporting students in their academic and professional development.
