Leadership & Vision

Messages from Leadership

Working with our students has been the greatest pleasure. We've built something truly special—a space where excellence isn't just taught, it's lived. From our classrooms to our grounds, every resource exists to help you become not just successful, but responsible and grounded.

Swami Dayanand Sarswati's ideals guide us: hard work, brotherhood, scientific thinking. Our legacy speaks for itself—achievers across business, sports, politics, and beyond.

I'd be lost without this team and their hunger to build something great. Join us. Let's shape your future together.

Dr. Puneet Aneja

Principal

A pioneer in quality education since 1946. We excel in academics, sports, and co-curricular activities, rooted in Swami Dayanand Sarswati's ideals of hard work, brotherhood, scientific thinking, and discipline. Our mission is to transform students into achievers who are self-reliant, progressive, socially responsible, and morally strong. We provide modern infrastructure—cutting-edge laboratories, high-tech facilities, vast grounds, and a dedicated Women Wing. Our alumni have excelled across business, politics, international sports, education, art, cinema, and theater. Join us—a unique portal blending tradition and modernity to launch your future.

Shri Vinod Bhardwaj

President, Managing Committee
