The Vision Behind Our Foundation

Education as the foundation of character.

Maharishi Dayanand Saraswati, in pursuance of Vedic values of tolerance, compassion, sharing and respect, founded Arya Samaj on April 10, 1875, at Mumbai and subsequently established its influence in Lahore in 1877.

His vision of education took the form of Vedic Schools and Gurukuls which placed emphasis on Vedic values, culture and religion, alongside the development of knowledge and character.

Maharishi Dayanand was a pioneer of social reform and an ardent advocate of Vedic culture. He insisted upon the physical, moral and social progress of humanity, placing education at the centre of character development.

According to this philosophy, the development of character is based on the achievement of knowledge. The aim of education is therefore not merely to provide information, but to give true knowledge to the individual and enable that person to conduct life properly.

His teachings emphasized that society must recognize education as a primary concern of the state. Following this philosophy, particularly the Eighth Principle of Arya Samaj , a number of dedicated Arya Samajists realized the need for an institution of higher learning in Nawanshahr Doaba.

“The aim of education is to give true knowledge to the individual and thus enable him to conduct properly.”

— Educational Philosophy

Our Journey

From a vision to an institution.

The vision of education and character development inspired generations of Arya Samajists to establish institutions dedicated to higher learning.

1875

Arya Samaj Founded

Maharishi Dayanand Saraswati founded Arya Samaj at Mumbai, beginning a movement centred around Vedic knowledge, truth, social reform and education.

1877

Arya Samaj in Lahore

The movement expanded to Lahore, strengthening the spread of Vedic values, education and social reform across the region.

VEDIC EDUCATION

Schools & Gurukuls

The educational vision took shape through Vedic Schools and Gurukuls, emphasizing knowledge, culture, values and the development of character.

1952

R.K. Arya College

As a result of the dedication of Arya Samajists to the cause of education, Radha Krishan Arya College was founded in Nawanshahr Doaba in 1952, carrying forward the vision of education, character and social progress.
